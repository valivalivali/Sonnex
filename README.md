# Sonnex

**AI-Powered Music DAW for macOS**

Sonnex is a digital audio workstation with a built-in AI assistant. Describe the music you want—chord progressions, melodies, drum patterns, basslines—and the AI creates the MIDI for you.

---

## Features

### AI Music Creation
- Chat with the AI to create music in natural language
- Generate chord progressions, melodies, drums, and basslines by describing them
- AI creates MIDI clips directly on your timeline
- Request changes, variations, or new ideas through conversation

### DAW Essentials
- Multi-track timeline for arranging audio and MIDI
- Piano roll editor for detailed MIDI note editing
- AudioUnit (AU) plugin support for instruments and effects
- BPM and tempo control
- Project save and load (.xdaw format)
- Audio export to WAV

---

## System Requirements

- **macOS**: 26.0 or later
- **Processor**: Apple Silicon only
- **RAM**: 8 GB minimum

---

## Getting Started

1. **Create a track** — Add a MIDI or Audio track from the left panel
2. **Open the AI chat** — Use the right panel to talk to the AI assistant
3. **Describe your music** — Tell the AI what you want (e.g., "create a sad piano chord progression in C minor")
4. **Refine and arrange** — Edit the generated MIDI in the piano roll, add more tracks, and build your song

### Common Questions

**Q: Why is there no sound from MIDI tracks?**  
A: Make sure you have an instrument plugin loaded on the track, or use the built-in sampler.

**Q: How do I use my own AU plugins?**  
A: Click the plugin browser button in the toolbar to browse and load AudioUnit instruments and effects installed on your Mac.

**Q: Can I export my project as audio?**  
A: Yes. Go to File → Export Audio (⌘⇧E) to export your project as a WAV file.

**Q: How do I talk to the AI?**  
A: Open the right panel (AI Assistant) and type your request in the chat. The AI can create tracks, generate MIDI, set tempo, and more.

**Q: The AI isn't responding.**  
A: Check that you have entered a valid API key in the AI settings (gear icon in the AI panel).
