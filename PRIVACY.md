# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: January 3, 2026**

## Overview

Sonnex ("the App") is a digital audio workstation for macOS. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the App accesses, how it's used, and your rights.

---

## Data We Collect

**We do not collect any personal data.**

Sonnex does not:
- Track your usage
- Collect analytics
- Store your information on external servers
- Require account creation

---

## Data Stored Locally

All your data stays on your device:

| Data | Storage Location |
|------|------------------|
| Projects (.xdaw files) | Your chosen save location |
| Exported audio (WAV) | Your chosen export location |
| API keys (if entered) | macOS Keychain (encrypted) |
| App preferences | Local app container |

---

## AI Features

Sonnex includes an AI assistant powered by Anthropic's Claude API.

### What happens when you use the AI:
- Your chat messages are sent to Anthropic's API servers for processing
- Anthropic processes your request and returns a response
- **You must provide your own API key** — Sonnex does not include or manage API keys

### What is NOT sent:
- Your audio files
- Your project files
- Any data beyond the current chat conversation

For Anthropic's privacy practices, see: [Anthropic Privacy Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/privacy)

---

## Third-Party Services

| Service | Purpose | Their Privacy Policy |
|---------|---------|---------------------|
| Anthropic (Claude API) | AI chat features | [anthropic.com/privacy](https://www.anthropic.com/privacy) |

Sonnex does not use any other third-party services, analytics, or tracking.

---

## Audio & Plugin Access

Sonnex accesses:
- **Audio files** you import or record (stored locally)
- **AudioUnit plugins** installed on your Mac (for instrument/effect processing)
- **Microphone** (only if you choose to record audio, with your permission)

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export or share it.

---

## Children's Privacy

Sonnex does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Since we don't collect any personal data, there is no age-specific data handling.

---

## Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device:
- **Delete your data** by removing project files and uninstalling the App
- **Access your data** directly from your file system
- **Control AI usage** by not entering an API key (AI features will be disabled)
